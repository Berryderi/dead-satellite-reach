Who you'd have to ask

The catalog gives a country or agency, not a company. Dead satellites stay under the state that registered them (Outer Space Treaty, Article VIII), so each one needs consent.

The satellites in reach

Fuel is the cheapest one-way trip inside your wait. Radar size is the catalog's cross-section, a rough guide to physical size.

What this leaves out

  • The fuel figure combines plane and altitude change for one direction only. It ignores the trip back, lining up inside the orbit, and catching a tumbling object.
  • Drift is modelled at constant rates. This is a desk estimate, not a mission design, and no spacecraft engineer has checked it yet.
  • "Dead" is one compiler's status code. Confirm each satellite with its owner.
